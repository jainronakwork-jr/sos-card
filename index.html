How to set it on your lock screen

Android: open the downloaded image in Photos or Gallery → ⋮ menu → Set as wallpaper → Lock screen.

iPhone: open it in Photos → Share → Use as Wallpaper → set as Lock Screen. Move the clock so it doesn't cover your details.

Also fill in your phone's own emergency info (iPhone: Health → Medical ID, Android: Settings → Safety & emergency). Responders know to look there too.

Cut along the edges, fold in the middle and keep it in your wallet behind your ID.
